Two bugs in training/torchrl_pg.py, both fixed in 5be4f33 ("Fix the TorchRL REINFORCE sampling and loss"):
1. No exploration: sessions were collected with env.rollout outside set_exploration_type(ExplorationType.RANDOM),
   and TorchRL 0.3.1 then takes the most likely action every time. Check: 1 distinct action in 200 draws
   (15 with RANDOM); 10 sessions used 4 distinct actions (14 with RANDOM).
2. Wrong actions in the loss: ReinforceLoss 0.3.1 re-runs the actor and overwrites the batch's taken actions
   with new samples (82 of 87 changed on a test batch), so its policy term scored actions that were never taken.
   Loss on the same sampled batch: +11.08770 (old) vs +11.09994 (plain and fixed).

Symptoms in this sweep: default-setting gap -0.20 vs plain (300 test users), seeds collapsing to about -2.0 in
settings 2 and 6, rank correlation with the plain sweep -0.02, range of setting means 0.92 (plain 0.24).
