A2C ran in two parts (2026-11-24):
- Settings 0-4 (15 runs) ran 17:10-17:29 at commit 1d10ac3. The sweep then stopped at setting 5 (entropy
  coefficient 0) with: KeyError 'loss_entropy' not found. TorchRL 0.3.1's A2CLoss leaves out the entropy term
  when its coefficient is 0 (entropy_bonus = entropy_bonus and entropy_coef), and the trainer added that term.
- Fixed in commit b571562 ("Fix the TorchRL A2C loss when the entropy coefficient is 0"): a missing entropy term
  counts as 0. Evidence (tests/test_torchrl.py): with a nonzero coefficient the fixed sum gives the same loss and
  gradients as the formula used before the fix, so settings 0-4 are unaffected; with coefficient 0 it matches the
  plain A2C loss. Settings 5-9 were smoke-tested (2,000 steps, 1 seed) before the resume.
- Settings 5-9 (15 runs) resumed at commit b571562, after the REINFORCE sweep:
  scratch/venv311/bin/python -m training.sweep --impl torchrl --algo a2c --runs 10 --steps 100000 --seeds 3 --workers 2
  (finished runs are skipped, so only settings 5-9 run). The crashed start left partial training logs for
  a2c_run06_seed0-2 and a2c_run07_seed0-1 (those runs were not in the summary); the resume overwrites them.
  Settings 5-9 smoke-tested again at 5be4f33 (2,000 steps, 1 seed): all ran without errors.
PPO's entropy-0 setting (run 6) was not affected: ClipPPOLoss keeps the entropy term with coefficient 0.
REINFORCE has no entropy-0 setting.
